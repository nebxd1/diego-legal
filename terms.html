Estimates, not advice

Diego counts days and applies published residency, tax-presence and visa rules to the trips you record. Its results are estimates only. They are not tax, legal, immigration or financial advice. Rules have exceptions, tie-breakers and recent changes that the app may not capture, and the results are only as accurate as the trips recorded. Before making decisions based on residency or tax status, consult a qualified professional.
